                  Ruling is pending.

    ====================================
    URGENCY LEVELS
    ====================================

    Displayed as a badge on the card -- separate from the lead score.

    ____________________________
    MORTGAGE FORECLOSURES
    ____________________________

    Mortgage foreclosures follow a predictable legal timeline with clear
    escalation points. Urgency rises as the case moves closer to a sheriff sale.

        HIGH      Writ Return
                      Sheriff has acted. Sale has run or is imminent.

        HIGH      Writ of Execution / Writ Issued
                      Sheriff sale is authorized and scheduled.

        HIGH      Sheriff Sale / Stay / Postponement
                      Sale is on the calendar, even if temporarily delayed.

        MEDIUM    Final Judgment
                      Court ruled for the lender. Sale process will begin.

        MEDIUM    Motion for Final Judgment
                      Judgment is pending. Sale process is close.

        NONE      All earlier stages


    ____________________________
    TAX LIEN FORECLOSURES
    ____________________________

    Tax lien foreclosures work differently from mortgage foreclosures.
    They are less structured, move on their own timeline, and do not have
    the same clear escalation points. For this reason, there is no HIGH
    urgency level for tax liens.

    The only stage worth flagging is default -- the owner has stopped
    engaging, which creates a potential window to approach them.

    Once a tax lien moves past default (Final Judgment, Writ, Redemption),
    the lien holder effectively owns the property. Anyone buying a tax lien
    is a sophisticated investor looking to profit -- not a motivated seller.
    These are no-go leads.

        MEDIUM    Redemption Order  (Motion/Order Fixing Amount)
                      Court has set a specific dollar amount and deadline
                      for the owner to pay off the lien. Owner has a clock
                      ticking and clearly can't or won't pay. Best window
                      to approach before the lien holder takes title.

        MEDIUM    Default stage
                      Owner went silent. Earlier window, less pressure.

        NONE      Late stage  (Final Judgment, Writ Issued, Writ Return)
                      Lien holder owns it. Do not pursue.

        NONE      All other tax lien stages

            Plaintiff name matches known tax lien investors:

            TAX LIEN, LIEN HOLDER, CERTES, TOWER,
            FUND LLC, CAPITAL LLC, INVESTORS LLC, HOLDINGS LLC

            COUNTY OF, STATE OF, CITY OF, TOWNSHIP OF, BOROUGH OF,
            MUNICIPAL, HOUSING AUTHORITY, REDEVELOPMENT

            Default for banks, servicers, and private lenders.

            Fallback for LLC, INC, CORP, TRUST, BANK, etc.
            when no other rule applies.



    ====================================
    COURT TERMS GLOSSARY
    ====================================

    Writ of Execution
        Court order authorizing the sheriff to conduct a sale.

    Alias Writ
        A renewed writ issued when the original expired without a sale.

    Writ Return
        Sheriff has returned the writ after the sale occurred or was attempted.

    Final Judgment
        Court ruled in the lender's favor. Owner has legally lost.

    Motion for Final Judgment  (MFJ)
        Lender's request asking the court to enter final judgment.

    Uncontested Order for Final Judgment
        Final judgment entered without opposition from the owner.

    Default / Request for Default
        Owner failed to respond to the complaint within the required time.

    Contested Answer
        Owner formally responded to the foreclosure complaint.

    Contested Answer w/ Counterclaim
        Owner responded and also filed their own claims against the lender.

    Notice of Appearance
        An attorney filed notice that they represent a party in the case.

    Stipulation of Settlement
        Both parties agreed to resolve the case (sale, payoff, modification).

    Stipulation of Dismissal
        Case formally dismissed, usually after settlement.

    Automatic Stay
        Bankruptcy filing that halts all foreclosure activity by federal law.

    Guardian Ad Litem
        Court-appointed representative for a party who cannot represent themselves.

    Motion to Reform Mortgage
        Request to correct an error in the original mortgage documents.

    Certification of Diligent Inquiry
        Lender's sworn statement that they tried and failed to locate the owner.

    14-Day Notice
        Required notice served to the owner before sheriff sale proceeds.

    Postponement Letter
        Sale was delayed, often at owner's request or by agreement.

    Motion / Order Fixing Amount
        Court sets the exact dollar amount needed to redeem (pay off) the property.

    Redemption
        Owner's right to reclaim the property by paying the full lien balance + fees.

    Additional Sums
        Extra amounts added to the debt: attorney fees, advances, taxes paid by lender.

    Limited Representation
        Attorney entered for a limited purpose only -- not full representation.

    Substitution of Attorney
        Owner changed lawyers mid-case.

    Deficiency Notice
        Notice that the sale proceeds didn't fully cover the debt.
        Owner may still owe the difference.
